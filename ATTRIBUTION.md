# Attribution

Square Packing Archive exists because of the unusually careful public record
maintained by the packing community.

- Erich Friedman's survey and original “Squares in Squares” collection provide
  the historical foundation, terminology, and many published bounds.
- David Ellsworth's current tracker provides high-precision reconstructions,
  SVG source data, corrected attribution, and continuing record updates.
- Each claim in `archive/manifest.json` links its authors and primary or tracker
  sources. A claim's presence does not imply endorsement by those authors.

Tracker pages and papers remain copyrighted by their respective owners. This
repository does not copy their prose or artwork. Configurations are normalized
from factual coordinate data and rendered by this project's own viewer.
